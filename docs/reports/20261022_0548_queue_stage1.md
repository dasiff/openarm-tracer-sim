## Questions for David

1. **R2c, the skill path's approach direction.** I implemented: a task point may carry a required approach direction (`"bin"`): then only that direction is tried; a point without one tries the directions the reach maps report first, then the others. The task points of the current scene carry none (any direction, as in the old search). The scripted pick and place approach along the robot's forward direction (bin 0). Should the points of the live plan carry bin 0? (It is stricter: the stance would have to support the forward approach at every point.)
2. **R2i, which objects count.** The occlusion check counts the robot and the unknown objects (the slots) as occluders; the static room and furniture stay in the reference image, because taking the whole room away changes the beaker's pixel count by about 3 % (depth buffer resolution) and made the TASK stance fail on noise. The IK path check and the clearance checks count every non-robot collidable geom except the beaker (the target). Is that the intent of "anything that hides a target"?
3. **The error stances are now collision-checked** (the old ladders only checked that the IK solves). The TASK stance passes either way; the stricter check could fail other stances. Keep?
4. **The clearance map** takes 30 s to build once per scene (70 furniture primitives, 489 arm surface points) and 7.7 s for the quick search at one heading (8 pedestal heights); it is an estimate with a 1 cm slack (it errs on the lenient side in 1 of 60 random stances by up to 43 mm, never by more than the full check then finds).
5. **Neck repeatability draws:** still 8 in this stage (the old rule): 27 solves, 16.7 s: R2e (stage 6) is what brings it under 10 s.
