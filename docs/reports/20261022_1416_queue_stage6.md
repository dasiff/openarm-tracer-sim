# Overnight queue, Stage 6: the new neck framing rule (behind `FRAMING_CFG["rule"]`, default "aim_centre")

Written for: David.

## What was built (row M151; design plan 14.1, M135)
- `FRAMING_CFG["rule"]`: "aim_centre" is today's rule (default, nothing changes); "least_movement": after parking, the least movement from the driving pose that frames every target with the 10 % margin (no aim term, no posture term; the cost is the movement from the start pose, `least_move_weight` 1.0), solved exactly: the simplex search as before, then a constrained polish (SLSQP: minimise the movement with every point inside by the margin and in front of the camera), because the optimum sits on the margin where the penalty search stopped imprecisely.
- `margins_at` and `reframe_or_keep` (src/neck_framing.py): at a null step the neck keeps its pose while every target is more than 5 % inside the image (`keep_margin`), otherwise it moves the least needed from where it is to restore 10 %.
- The full check's repeatability uses 2 jiggled solves under the new rule (`FULLCHECK_CFG["neck_draws_least_movement"]`), 8 under the old; the full check's cache key includes the framing settings.

## Measurements (`experiments/neck_rule_test.py`, 10/10; at the handoff pose, the staging stance with the arms at home; images `docs/reports/neck_rule_old_vs_new.png`, left old, right new, and the two single images)

|---|---|---|
| neck movement from the driving pose | 0.993 rad | 0.796 rad |
| worst margin | 10.00 % | 10.00 % |
| aim error | 0.90 deg | 3.26 deg |
| camera position (m) | 0.8107, 0.8458, 1.5202 | 0.7985, 0.8385, 1.4906 |
| time per framing | 0.76 s and 1.13 s (two runs, the machine's speed varied) | 0.73 s and 1.36 s |
| repeatability (largest joint change over jiggled solves, 0.1 degree on every arm joint) | 0.0070 rad (8 draws) | 0.0028 rad (2 draws), 0.0044 rad (8 draws) |
| neck contacts | none | none |

Both images show the beaker, the hotplate and both grippers; the new one is slightly closer. Null step: a pose at the 10 % margin is kept; targets moved sideways by 1 cm (margin 8.5 %) keep the pose; by 4 cm (margin 4.0 %) the neck moves 0.099 rad and restores 10 %, where the old rule from scratch would move 0.29 to 0.31 rad.

The first version (simplex only) gave a repeatability of 0.078 rad over 2 draws, above the 0.06 rad limit of neck_once_test; the limit was not loosened, the solver was made exact (the polish above).
