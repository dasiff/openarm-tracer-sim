## 7. The VLM refresh (E4): blocked
What the cache holds: 12 files = 6 replies (the plan, the labeling, and 4 VLM checks) plus 4 images; the 4 VLM replies are for the OLD neck-mounted images and no longer match. The refresh needs only 2 new VLM calls per episode (the grip check and the release check). Attempts: 18:21 and 18:27 gave HTTP 503 after all 7 tries (the service was overloaded); 22:45 gave HTTP 429 `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`, which the pipeline classifies as the daily quota (rule A1: a quota error stops, no retry; the run ends PIPELINE_PROBLEM with "checker unavailable: daily quota"). So **no new verdict exists, and the 12 (really 2 new + 4 old) verdicts next to the oracle's cannot be reported yet.** Everything else in these runs worked: the plan and labeling replayed from the cache, the pipeline ran through staging and the pick (oracle PASS at t = 57.2 s) and the recording replayed bit-identically. To finish: after the quota resets, `python experiments/mpvi_text_test.py --mode cache_or_live --checker vlm` (about 3 minutes, 2 API calls); then `mpvi_text_test` in the suite passes from the cache, and the verdicts are in its summary next to the oracle's.
