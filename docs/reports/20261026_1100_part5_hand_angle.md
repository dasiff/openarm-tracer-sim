## 7. Decisions log row M183, word for word (docs/mpvi_decisions.md)
| M183 | 2026-10-26 | The hand's angle during the place, and the full check tests what the robot does (David's decision, Oct 26; principle: the full check must test exactly what the robot will do, at the robot's own pass mark). (a) The place stops while holding (carry stop OVER, lowering and release stops, and the back-off) use one hand angle theta: the hand stays level (no tilt), turned by theta about the vertical axis through the beaker's centre (the destination's centre); the pad centre and the approach direction turn with it, the back-off goes out along the turned approach direction; theta = 0 is the old straight forward. (b) The full check (`FullCheck._place_window`, src/full_check.py), for each candidate spot: theta in `place_turn_step_deg` = 5 degree steps all the way round (0 to 355). At each theta every place stop must be reached within the episode's own waypoint tolerance (PICK_PLACE_CFG `tol_m` 3 mm and `tol_rad` 2 degrees, the exact rotation) and pass the full check's collision tests (arm against the robot, hand clearance 10 mm, arm clearance 0 to the furniture and objects, no elbow flip along a path), at the nominal stance and at each of the 8 error stances. The working window is a run of consecutive working thetas (around the circle); the widest is chosen and the middle theta of it. If the widest window spans under `place_turn_min_window_deg` = 10 degrees (first to last working theta) a narrower window is too fragile and the spot fails the full check with the failure "place_angle_window" and the message "no place angle window". (c) The pick stops (grasp band, OVER, the standoff, the lift, the back-off) are tested at exactly the skill's angle (level hand, approach straight along the robot's forward direction, the roll of the zombie pose's closing axis as `pad_rotation`) with the same tolerances: the turn and tilt allowance (the grasp-bin rule: level within 5 degrees, direction within 15 degrees of a bin centre) is removed for the skill's stops. Points with no "pose" key (full_check_test's legacy 12 points) are still tested as grasps in a bin. The old "above" points (10 cm above the set-down) are no longer tested: the skill has no such stop (its OVER is at the carry height). (d) The stance selection result carries theta: `run["place_theta_deg"]` in the report and `place_theta_deg` on the place segment of the plan; `plan_place` (src/mpvi_modules.py) turns the hand smoothly from the grasp angle to theta during the carry move (the OVER move's rotation slerp, limited by `turn_rad_s`) and uses theta for the lowering, release and back-off stops; no theta (navigation off, the reference stance) means 0, today's behaviour. New settings (FULLCHECK_CFG): `place_turn_step_deg` 5, `place_turn_min_window_deg` 10 (David), `pose_ik_iterations` 150 (the IK iterations of an exact-pose stop: my choice, PENDING David). Known differences, not modelled: the check puts the pad centre on the beaker's axis while the skill's measured pad offset in the test scenes is about 9 mm; the check's IK is a damped least squares solver, not mink with its speed limit and sag; the held beaker is not in the collision tests (C0 a). Measured on the test scene (selection, 41 to 50 s): normal layout spot (0.755, 1.145) window -50 to 0 degrees, theta -25; moved layout spot (0.635, 1.135) window -50 to -5 degrees, theta -25. | David 2026-10-26 |
