## Step 4: how the full check and the robot's solver start (read only; in the design plan's Later list, NOT DECIDED)
- **The full check** (`src/full_check.py`, `_solve_stance` and `_solve_pose_point`): at each stop it first tries WARM starts: the solution of the previous stop of the SAME path (the stops of one skill path are solved in order, and the elbow-flip test compares against it) and, for an error stance, the solution of the same stop at the nominal stance (over the place turns, the previous turn's nominal solution). Only if no warm start gives an admissible solution (reached within the tolerance, no collision, clear, no elbow flip) does it add `ik_seeds` RANDOM starts, uniform over the joint ranges. The first stop of each path at the nominal stance has no warm start, so it starts from random. It never starts from the arm's current pose or from the home pose.
- **The robot's solver** (mink, `src/mink_arm.py`): each skill starts from the arm's MEASURED joints (`reset`), then moves its command a small velocity step per tick (speed limited to 0.5 rad/s, with collision avoidance and a weak posture task toward the home pose that picks the elbow), so each move continues from the one before.
- Consequence for your concern: the first stop of each path, and any failure that needs the random phase, can in principle find an arm shape reached from nowhere near where the robot is; the warm starts are neighbourhood starts. A neighbourhood start with random restarts only as a diagnostic is a change of the first-stop rule, not of the rest. Written into the design plan as a small item, not decided.

## Step 6: handoff weight measurement (measurement only)
**Method** (`src/weight_estimate.py`, `experiments/handoff_weight_measure.py`; logs `data/logs/handoff_20261026_134026_*`, raw results `data/handoff_weight/20261026_134026/*/result.json`). Normal layout, one episode per mass, liquid mass changed in the harness only (`load_task(beaker_cfg=...)`; task_config untouched); glass 0.10 kg. Signals used: measured angles, joint speeds, applied motor torque, plus the arm's own gravity torque from the arm's bodies (`gravity_ff`); the true mass is read from the model only after the run.
- a. **Still:** all 7 left-arm joints below **0.005 rad/s** (4 times stricter than the module's own settled speed of 0.02), for a **0.30 s window**, and the reading is the mean over that window (45 ticks at 150 Hz).
- b. Extra torque = applied torque minus the arm's own gravity torque. c. Straight-down force at the pad centre through the Jacobian, least squares, weight = force / g. d. Sanity: a force in any direction; its angle from straight down; the share of the extra torque each fit leaves unexplained (norm of the residual over the norm of the extra torque).
- **Handoff points as run:** the pick's handoff is the end of the pick module (its last step, CHECK, with the beaker lifted; the arm is still at the end of UP). The place module goes straight from the back-off to the return home without stopping, so the arm is never still at the end of BACK; the place handoff is the end of the whole place module (after the release, the back-off and the return to the zombie pose; hand empty). I also took an extra reading at the end of BACK with a looser stillness (0.02 rad/s, 0.2 s).

- **Time to be still.** At 0.15 and 0.20 kg the arm was already below 0.005 rad/s when the pick's CHECK step ended: the still stretch started 0.33 and 0.32 s before it, and (checked for the 0.20 kg total) about 0.13 s before the end of the planned minimum-jerk UP motion, so the reading was taken at once (no extra wait; the window used data from before the handoff). At the end of the place: still for 1.15 s already when the module finished (0 s after the end of its motion). At the end of BACK (looser speed): still 0.26 s after the planned motion end.
- **Estimate quality.** The held readings are within 2 to 6 % and a bit high. The fit is not clean: the load acts 14 to 19 degrees off straight down and the straight-down fit leaves 15 to 17 % of the extra torque unexplained. With the empty hand the same method reads +2.3 to +2.5 g, but its free fit says a horizontal force of about 0.6 N with 70 % unexplained, i.e. a noise floor in the horizontal direction (the servos' steady-state error times the stiffness, which the vertical fit mostly ignores); so the 15 to 19 degree tilt of the held fits is partly that floor and partly the beaker's centre of mass not being at the pad centre (the fit has no couple). A poor fit does flag something besides a pure hanging weight here, but at these masses the weight comes out right regardless. Extra reading at the end of BACK, hand empty: -2.2 to -2.6 g, fit 100 % unexplained (nothing to explain), fine.
- **0.30 kg.** The run fails on its own, before the weight matters: the pick works, but the place's DOWN waypoint is not reached after 9 s (4.4 mm and 0.2 degrees short, tolerance 3 mm / 2 degrees), the recovery starts, and the beaker falls out when the recovery's return home moves the arm at 0.5 rad/s (finger opening drops to 2.7 mm at t = 82 s; the drop monitor fires at 81.7 s; the plan then fails with "the hand holds nothing"). The slip time series (re-run of this one case with a time series added, same result; `data/handoff_weight/20261026_134026/liquid_0.20_series/result.json`): during the lift the beaker moves 11 to 14 mm in the pad frame (t = 58.1 to 60.1 s) and stays within 9 to 16 mm through the carry and lowering; the fingers stay at 37.5 mm opening; it moves 45 to 50 mm in the first recovery step and about 98 to 116 mm when it is lost. The arm creeps at 0.01 to 0.04 rad/s at the end of the lift (the next step starts 0.5 s later), so my 0.005 rad/s window never opens: **no handoff reading at 0.30 kg**. I did not tune the threshold to get one.
- **Grip movement is not growing with mass** (11.7, 24.5 and about 14 mm before the recovery for 200, 150 and 300 g): it happens during the lift at all masses and includes the beaker tilting about the pads, because the position I track is the beaker's base centre, 53 mm from the pad centre. So it says "the beaker shifts and tilts in the grip when lifted", not "slides out", until the recovery's fast move at 300 g. I did not log the beaker's tilt separately.
- **What this suggests (not acted on):** the estimator is accurate when the arm is still at the handoff. At 0.30 kg the limit is the scripted skill and the grip (the sag correction cannot reach the 3 mm tolerance at the place's DOWN), not the estimate. Also a modest systematic +2 to +6 % at the pick and a roughly 2 to 3 g noise floor for the empty hand.
