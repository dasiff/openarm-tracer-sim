### a. Where on each pad the beaker touches
- **The pads.** Each finger's pad is a flat patch of the collision mesh (the vertices within 1 mm of its inner face, the way `src/gripper_geometry` finds the pad centre): **12.4 mm along the approach by 23.7 mm across (vertical)**, only 7 mesh vertices.
- **At the grasp** (end of closing, tilt 0.7 degrees): left finger: 2 contact points, both **2.4 mm from the tip edge** (3.8 mm toward the tip from the pad centre), spread 4.8 mm across (from -3.0 to +1.9 mm about the middle height), 7.8 N, on beaker wall segment 12; right finger: 1 contact point, **2.3 mm from the tip edge**, 2.9 mm below the middle height, 8.2 N, on wall segment 0. So at the grasp both fingers hold the beaker in the tip-most fifth of the pad's length, near the middle of its height.
- **At the handoff** (tilt 11.7 degrees): left finger 3 points, one still 2.2 mm from the tip and one at the far corner (12.4 mm from the tip, 12.2 mm up); right finger 1 point, 2.2 mm from the tip, 2.8 mm below the middle.
- **Over the lift and the carry** (1,065 ticks): right finger's contacts stay between **0.1 and 2.7 mm from the tip edge** (an edge contact), 5.8 mm below to 0.3 mm above the middle height; left finger's go from 1.0 to 12.8 mm from the tip and from 5.8 mm below to 15.3 mm above the middle (so to the pad's far corner during the tilt).
- **Size of each contact patch:** MuJoCo gives point contacts, so the patch is the spread of the points: 1 to 3 points per finger at any moment, at most about 5 mm apart at the grasp; the contact is a point or a short line, not an area.

### b. How deep the hand went, and why it stopped there
- **Depth:** at the grasp the beaker's axis passes **0.7 mm** from the pad centre (0.7 along the approach, 0.2 along the closing axis, 0.0 across); at the handoff 1.3 mm. "Centred in the pads" is therefore already true. The fingertips (the tip edge of the pads) are 6.5 mm past the axis at the grasp.
- **How the target is defined** (src/mpvi_modules.py `plan_pick`): the IN waypoint puts the **pad centre** (the centre of the pad patch found by `gripper_geometry`, 6.2 mm back from the tip edge) on the beaker's axis at the grasp height: `move("IN", [b[0], b[1], hg], ...)` with `b` the beaker's position (its axis) and `hg = bottom + grasp_height_frac x height`. Not the fingertips. **The approach did not stop early:** IN ended 1.09 mm and 0.2 degrees from its target (tolerance 3 mm / 2 degrees; from the Part C step 1 runs). So the depth is as the skill defines it.
- **The 9 mm pad offset of M183:** not reproduced as a pad-to-axis distance here (0.7 to 1.3 mm). What is about 9 to 11 mm is the beaker's bottom centre against the pad centre when the beaker is tilted: the pad is 53.9 mm above the bottom and 53.9 x sin 11.7 degrees = 10.9 mm; and the place code measures its offset from the beaker's bottom centre (`off = p - b`). So I think the 9 mm is the tilt, not a hand that is offset (interpretation, not tested).

### c. The pad geometry and friction in the sim, against the real gripper
- **Sim, from the compiled model:** finger collision geoms are meshes (`openarm_left_left_finger_collision` uses mesh `finger_collision`; `openarm_left_right_finger_collision` uses `finger_collision_flip`), geom friction `[1, 0.01, 0.01]` (sliding, torsional, rolling), `condim 3`, `solref [0.005, 1]`, `solimp [0.9, 0.95, 0.001, 0.5, 2]` (file deps/OpenArm-Combined/combined_robot.xml, line 43: `<geom condim="3" conaffinity="1" priority="1" group="3" solref="0.005 1" friction="1 0.01 0.01" />`).
- **The beaker's contacts use explicit pairs** (`src/beaker.py`, BEAKER_CFG `contact_friction`): every finger and palm geom against every wall and bottom segment, **`condim 3`, friction `[0.4, 0.4, 0.005, 0.0001, 0.0001]`**. With `condim 3` MuJoCo uses only the sliding friction (0.4); the torsional (0.005) and rolling (0.0001) entries are ignored. **Twisting friction across the contact is NOT modelled.** Other model facts: pyramidal friction cone, impratio 1, `noslip_iterations` 10.
- **The beaker** is 24 wall segments (flat facets 15 degrees apart) and a bottom disk; the wall segments touched are 12 and 0, exactly opposite.
- **The real gripper's description** (data/import_cache/openarm_description, the pinch gripper): `assets/end_effector/pinch_gripper/config/link/collisions.yaml` gives only meshes, e.g. for link1 `mesh: "package://openarm_description/assets/end_effector/pinch_gripper/meshes/collision/finger_inner.stl"` with scale 1 (other collision meshes `finger_outer.stl`, `ee_base_link.stl`); the urdf (`pinch_gripper_standalone.urdf.xacro`) and the yaml files carry **no friction, no pad material and no pad size**. A search of the description package and the upstream MuJoCo files finds friction only in the MuJoCo xml files (`friction="1 0.01 0.01"`, `condim="3"`), which are the sim's own. So the real pad's friction and softness are not in the repo; the sim's pad shape is the real finger's collision mesh.

### d. Room between the beaker and the palm
On a copy of the state at the end of the closing, I moved the beaker toward the palm (the hand going deeper) by 0 to 30 mm and measured the smallest distance of the beaker's geoms to the palm / hand body and to the other arm parts:

| hand deeper by | beaker to palm / hand body | beaker to fingers | beaker to other arm parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 mm (as is) | 42.3 mm | 0 (the pads) | 58.1 mm |
| 10 mm | 32.3 mm | 0 | 48.1 mm |
| 20 mm | 22.3 mm | 1.5 mm | 38.1 mm |
| 30 mm | 12.3 mm | 3.0 mm | 28.1 mm |

There is at least 30 mm of room to go deeper before the beaker would be 12 mm from the palm (about 42 mm now). The pad centre is already on the axis, so going deeper would put the beaker toward the pad's root, past the centre, not "centre it".

### e. The grasp height against the balance point
Pad centre 53.1 mm above the beaker's bottom (the target is 0.525 x 100 mm = 52.5 mm; contacts at 50 to 53 mm). The beaker's centre of mass (glass 0.10 kg plus the liquid's 0.10 kg low in the beaker) is **29.9 mm** above the bottom, so the pinch is **23.2 mm above the centre of mass** (horizontally the centre of mass is 0.1 mm from the pad centre at the grasp, 3.6 mm by the handoff when the beaker has swung). The empty glass alone would have its centre of mass at 43 % of the height (43 mm); the liquid lowers it.

### f. My reading of the cause (interpretation, not tested)
1. The hand is deep enough and centred; the contact positions are set by the pads and the beaker's shape: a small flat pad (12.4 mm long) against a 24-sided beaker whose facets touch it at the facet's edge on the tip side, 2.3 mm from the tip edge. Your tip hypothesis holds for the contact point on the pad (near the tip, an edge contact for the right finger), but not for the hand's depth.
2. Both contacts sit toward the tips, so the pinch line passes about 4.5 mm in front of the beaker's axis (167 degrees instead of 180 as seen from the axis).
3. The pinch is 23 mm above the centre of mass, and the contacts have no twisting friction (condim 3), so the beaker swings about the pinch line like a pendulum; its resting tilt, atan(4.5 / 23), is 11 degrees, matching the 11.7 degrees measured at 200 g, and the swing reaches 26 degrees at 300 g.
What would test it (not done, it changes behavior): condim 4 with a torsional friction, a rounder beaker (more segments), or a grasp height nearer the centre of mass; each would change the tilt if this reading is right.
