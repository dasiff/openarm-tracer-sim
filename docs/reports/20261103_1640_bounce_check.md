# Bounce check after the held-weight changes (lift-off weighing, ramps, calmer sag)

Date: 2026-11-03. Branch `held-weight-ramp-sag-off`, HEAD `29b8583`. Measurement only: no code, setting or default was changed, and the repo was not edited, committed or pushed.

## Result in one paragraph

Three simulated runs (beakers of 150, 200 and 250 g) all completed the full pick and place with no failed step. The big bounce from Nov 1 is much smaller, but it is not under the 3 mm line yet. In the pick's lift (the UP step) the hand rises about 4 mm with the 150 g and 200 g beakers and about 1.9 mm with the 250 g beaker. During the place's DOWN step the hand moves about 4.1 to 4.4 mm below the plan and about 4.8 to 5.0 mm above it, so the swing there is about 9 mm. Both are over the 3 mm line. The lift-off weight estimate reads too high for the 150 g and 200 g beakers (+17 % and +12 %) and almost exact for the 250 g beaker (+0.2 %).

## Terms used

- **Hand (pad) height:** the height of the centre of the two finger pads, in metres. Called "pad z" in the data.
- **Reference:** the height the planned path says the hand should be at. The difference "hand minus reference" is called **e** here, in millimetres. This is the same kind of number as the Nov 1 "18.4 mm below plan".
- **Lift-off:** the moment the beaker leaves the table, seen as the hand rising 0.3 mm above its level before the weighing started.
- **Held mass:** the weight the arm is told the beaker has. The arm uses it to add upward force so the beaker does not pull the arm down.
- **Ramp:** the held mass is changed slowly over 1.0 s with a smooth (minimum-jerk) curve, instead of in one step.
- **Sag correction:** an integral term that nudges the path to cancel how far the arm droops under the beaker. "Frozen" means it does not learn while a mass ramp runs.
- **Feedforward:** the torque the controller adds in advance to hold the arm against gravity and the beaker's weight.

## Measured results

Pass line (David): rise, drop and swing each under 3 mm, for 150, 200 and 250 g.

Definitions used here (from the per-tick CSV):

- **UP rise / UP drop:** the largest and smallest value of e in the window from 150 ticks before lift-off to 450 ticks after it. This window holds the pick's UP lift.
- **Place DOWN:** the largest and smallest e over the whole held period (first tick with held mass above zero to the end of the place OPEN step).

The metrics script's own "metric 1" (rise, drop, swing over 200 ticks after the first held-mass tick) is **not** used here. Its window closes at tick 11381, about 330 ticks before lift-off, so it does not cover the bounce. It reports only 0.07 to 0.09 mm, which says nothing about the event.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 150 g | 11707 (79.45 s) | +4.13 mm | -2.14 mm | 6.27 mm | +4.89 mm | -4.11 mm | 9.00 mm | No (rise 4.1, place swing 9.0) |
| 200 g | 11851 (80.41 s) | +4.03 mm | -2.12 mm | 6.15 mm | +4.99 mm | -4.40 mm | 9.39 mm | No (rise 4.0, place swing 9.4) |

Reference for the Nov 1 case: the hand rose 17.7 mm and dropped to 18.4 mm below plan. So the rise is about 4 times smaller at 150 g and 200 g and about 9 times smaller at 250 g, but still above 3 mm.

Time of the extremes (from the CSV): the UP maxima are at ticks 11802 (150 g), 11947 (200 g) and 12204 (250 g); the place DOWN extremes are at ticks 12458 to 12514 (150 g), 12602 to 12659 (200 g) and 12679 to 12737 (250 g). All are inside the place DOWN step.

## Lift-off weight estimate (reported, not fixed)

The estimate is the mass at detection minus (pad rise divided by hand height per kg). Hand height per kg is 0.08984 m/kg for all three runs.

| True beaker | Raw at detection | Corrected (used) | Error of corrected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.150 kg | 0.1791 kg (+29.1 g) | 0.1755 kg | +25.5 g, **+17.0 %** |
| 0.200 kg | 0.2280 kg (+28.0 g) | 0.2245 kg | +24.5 g, **+12.3 %** |
| 0.250 kg | 0.2538 kg (+3.8 g) | 0.2504 kg | +0.4 g, **+0.2 %** |

How it shows up: the 150 g and 200 g estimates are high by about 25 g. That is about 0.24 N of extra upward force that the feedforward adds, so the arm is pushed up while the beaker is still held. The 250 g estimate is nearly exact, so the same error does not appear there. The run data do not say why the raw error is about 28 g for two beakers and about 4 g for the third. This is recorded as an open question, not explained here.

## Other facts from the runs

- Lift-off came after 3.5 s (150 g), 4.5 s (200 g) and 5.0 s (250 g) of the ramp, all before the 1.0 kg cap.
- Every step passed on its first attempt (0 FAILED lines). Outcome: SUCCESS for all three runs.
- The 1.0 s ramp-in starts at lift-off detection, and the UP step starts at the same time (UP at 79.45 s, detection 79.447 s for 150 g; the same pattern in the other two runs). So the UP lift, where the rise above happens, runs during the ramp-in. The sag correction is frozen during that ramp by design (code path above). The per-tick `frozen` column was not checked tick by tick in this report.
