## What changed

- `src/control_loop.py`:
  - `applied()` shows the arm targets the sources commanded. The new `hold_arm_now()` gives the held targets (after the trim and the bridge).
  - In the command loop, each arms command's targets go into `src_arm_targets` before the filter, the trim and the bridge, for every mode. The arms still receive the corrected targets through the hold, unchanged.
  - The filter's first-call reference keeps its old value: the braking layer's view when braking is on, the held targets otherwise.
  - `BRIDGE_ACCEPTED_LOG` gains `limited_L` and `limited_R` (logging only): the bridge's own command after its rate limit.
- `experiments/sag_feedback_test.py` (new, 8 checks).
- `experiments/command_bridge_test.py`: observes the bridge through `limited_L`; case 1 passes `{"enabled": False}` explicitly (the bridge is on by default, so case 1 was already failing before this change).
- `docs/mpvi_decisions.md`: row SF-119. `docs/design_plan.md`: SF-119 entry.

The leaky fade, the failed-solve rule and the warm-start reset are unchanged.
