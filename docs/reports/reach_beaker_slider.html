Each floor spot (the robot's base origin) is coloured by how many of the 12 approach bins reach the beaker's grasp point from that spot, at the pedestal command on the slider. White = spot is over the bench top (not allowed). Grey = no bin reaches. Bench outline in white; the beaker is the red cross.

Pedestal command x is in metres (the stance search's grid). The readout also gives x minus the bench top height (0.96 m), since that was asked for; it is not used in the maps.
